# **App Name**: Coastal Sands Retreat

## Core Features:

- Online Booking: Enable direct room booking through a user-friendly online form with integrated payment gateway.
- Room Showcase: Detailed view for each room and suite with high-quality photos and descriptions of amenities.
- Experiences Highlight: Showcase the various hotel experiences and local tourist activities through descriptive text and photos. Leverage a generative AI tool to personalize experience recommendations based on guest preferences (e.g., type of traveler, interests, budget).
- Dynamic Pricing: Manage pricing options for both local and international guests and enable dynamic adjustments.
- Virtual Concierge: Integrate a WhatsApp button for instant customer support and query resolution.
- Guest Reviews: Display testimonials and ratings to build trust and showcase guest satisfaction.
- Interactive Map: Integrate Google Maps for location accuracy and directions, with custom markers for key attractions.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Ocean blue (#468B97) to evoke the Kenyan coastal atmosphere.
- Background color: Sandy beige (#F2E7D5), a desaturated tint of the primary.
- Accent color: Palm green (#77BFA3) for highlights and interactive elements, an analogous color that evokes the Kenyan flora.
- Headline font: 'Playfair', a modern serif with an elegant feel; body font: 'PT Sans', a humanist sans-serif appropriate for comfortable reading.
- Custom, elegant line icons representing various hotel amenities and local attractions.
- Mobile-first design with a grid layout ensuring responsiveness and easy navigation on all devices.
- Subtle transitions and parallax scrolling effects to enhance the visual experience and create depth.
